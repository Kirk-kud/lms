# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Church members across multiple university campuses including Ashesi, KNUST, University of Mines and Technology (UMaT), and University of Legon, among other branches. Students access the app between or after class sessions, typically on mobile or laptop, to check assignments and track their progress. Pastors use it to manage classes, post content, and review individual submissions.

## Product Purpose

A discipleship learning management system for Love Inc. Members join structured group study programs, each with individual assignments. Success means a member completing a class having engaged seriously with the material and grown in their faith walk. Pastors can see who is keeping up and follow up personally.

## Brand Personality

Bold, communal, purposeful. The brand carries confidence without being cold — it belongs to a real community of people, not a platform. Voice is direct and warm, the way a pastor addresses their congregation.

## Anti-references

- Generic LMS platforms (Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle) — institutional, sterile, impersonal
- AI-generated church apps — soft gradients, stock photography, generic spirituality
- Corporate HR or onboarding tools — task-manager aesthetics that feel like work
- Overly "designed" faith-tech startups — polished to the point of feeling inauthentic

## Design Principles

1. **Community is the product.** Real photos from gatherings are content, not decoration. The interface makes space for them.
2. **Bold, not busy.** The brand identity is strong and graphic. The UI should have the same confidence: clear hierarchy, no visual noise.
3. **Personal accountability, not surveillance.** Progress tracking should feel encouraging, not monitored. Copy and UI patterns should reflect care, not metrics.
4. **Built for this community.** Every design choice should feel specific to Love Inc — not a template that could belong to anyone.
5. **Helvetica discipline.** Typography follows the brand: heavy weight for hierarchy, restrained for body. No decorative type.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Community photo usage should never make members feel excluded. Light mode primary (used in daylight, on campus).
